Your adventure

The world map is controlled by the host. Pipes and level exits bring both players together.

Better together.

One person creates a room and shares its two-letter or number code. The friend joins as Luigi, right where Mario is playing.

Use the touch buttons, a gamepad, or your keyboard: arrows to move, Z / Space to jump, X to run and hold, Enter to start or pause.

Optional weapons: use the Rocket, Sniper, and Assault toggles below Select and Start (keyboard: 1, 2, 3). Tap the selected weapon again to put it away. Hold B / X to fire; up or down aims vertically, and combining a direction aims diagonally. Rockets break ordinary bricks and ice blocks with splash damage. Sniper shots pierce enemies, while the assault rifle fires rapidly. Shots stop at solid terrain. Your partner, power-ups and level mechanisms are protected.

Each player has their own camera. Jump on each other for a boost. If a player dies, they reappear beside their partner. The host chooses levels on the map; entering a pipe or finishing a level takes both players along.

This original-ROM co-op patch is experimental. Some special enemies and scripted rooms may need Regroup from the menu. Save progress before attempting a difficult level. The host must keep the game visible; backgrounding a phone pauses its browser.

Android connection trouble? In Android Settings > Apps > your browser > Permissions, allow Nearby devices / Local network, then reopen the game. Android 17 and newer can block same-Wi-Fi connections until this is allowed. Also allow local network access if your browser asks.

Connection settings

Normally these can stay blank. A TURN relay can help if your networks block direct connections.
